About IDR

Industry-led.
Academically grounded.

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute dedicated to developing the next generation of digital, cyber, and technology risk practitioners.

We specialise in preparing professionals for roles in regulated, high-consequence environments — where the stakes are highest and expertise is paramount.

Strategic UK University Partner
01 / MISSION

Bridging Academia & Industry

Through our strategic partnership with leading UK universities, we bridge the gap between academic insight and real-world application, combining rigorous theoretical foundations with hands-on experience on live projects.

02 / APPROACH

High-Consequence Readiness

Our graduates aren't just knowledgeable — they're immediately effective. We train practitioners for environments where failure is not an option, across financial services, critical infrastructure, and regulated sectors.

03 / INNOVATION

Advancing the Field

Beyond training, IDR actively develops intellectual property, future risk models, and AI governance frameworks — contributing to the evolution of digital risk as a discipline.
